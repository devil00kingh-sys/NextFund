FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Tap a question to reveal the answer.

What is the funding range at NXTFund?
Our funding ranges from seed rounds of approximately ₹1–2 Cr to Series A/B rounds of ₹6–10+ Cr depending on the startup's stage, sector and growth potential.
How do I apply to Celebso Startup School?
You can apply directly through the Startup School page. Click "Apply Now" on the admissions banner, complete the application form, and the admissions team will guide you through the next steps.
Who can apply for funding?
Early-stage founders from anywhere in the world with a strong team, a scalable product and a clear vision can apply. We evaluate every application on merit.
How can I track my application?
After submitting your application you'll receive a confirmation email with a reference ID. Our team will contact you with updates, or you can reach us directly via support@celebso.com.
Can I book a visit to Startup Valley?
Yes. You can book a campus visit through the school page or contact us at +91 97846 26443 to schedule a tour of Startup Valley Jaipur.
How long does it take to hear back?
Most applicants hear back within 5–7 business days. If you haven't received a response, feel free to follow up via email or live chat.
